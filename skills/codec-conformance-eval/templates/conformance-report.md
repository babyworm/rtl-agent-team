## Interpretation

- **Mandatory streams**: JVET and JCTVC official conformance streams MUST all pass for decoder
  to be considered conformant. Any mandatory failure means the decoder has algorithmic bugs.
- **Optional streams**: 3rd-party streams (Allegro, Elecard, etc.) provide additional coverage
  but do not affect the overall conformance verdict.
- **Comparison mode**: MD5 is fastest; bitexact reports exact mismatch location; psnr-threshold
  allows approximate matching for floating-point rounding tolerance.
